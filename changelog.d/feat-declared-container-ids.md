### Changed

- **BREAKING: container accounts and groups take declared numbers from the deployment's id block** (`models/agent_spec.py` `IdBlock`, `docker/scripts/start.py`, `docker/Dockerfile`, policy `container_operations` 6.2). A container without user-namespace remapping shares the host's id space, and order-assigned numbers landed on the host's own people: measured on a two-deployment host, container 1000 was the host's first login and the seats were its other users. agents.yaml now requires `ids: {base: N}` (a multiple of 1000, at least 20000) and a `uid` per agent in `base+100` to `+899`; the image requires the same `MACEFF_ID_BASE`, puts `admin` at `base`, the amail accounts at `base+998/999`, and removes the base image's `ubuntu` account. Start-up creates each group and account at its number and stops, naming both numbers, on an account, group or persisted home that holds another, or an image built for another block; moving an existing deployment is the renumber step's job, never a silent repair. Twenty-five tests (`test_declared_ids.py`).
