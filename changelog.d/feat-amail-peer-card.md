### Added

- **`macf_tools amail peer card`: what a peer's operator needs to declare this broker** (`amail/peer_setup.py`, amail policy 2.1.2): the mail domain, the declared listener's address and port, and for each agent on the card its calling card, public signing key and account, read from the operator's broker configuration through the broker's own loader. Prints the registry line the peer's operator runs as root, and the card itself as one line of JSON that `parse_card` reads back under the same rules the declarations enforce. A key comes from the registry first and, for the agent's own card only, from its own key file; an agent with neither is on the card with a note that its mail reads unverified. A broker with no `peer_listen` has no card. It carries no secret and changes nothing.
