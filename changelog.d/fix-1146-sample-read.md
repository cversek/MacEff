### Fixed

- **The prompt hook's budget notice reads the horizon's samples from an hourly carried copy** (`macf/budget.py`, #1146): the smoothed rate needs eight hours of `budget_sampled` events (four time constants), and reading them back through a dense log was the whole of the notice's remaining cost, 0.2 to 0.4 s on a large log. The prompt hook now writes a `budget_samples_carried` event holding those samples when there is none under an hour old, and every later read of the horizon stops at it and adds the samples after it; a carry of a shorter horizon is ignored, and only the prompt hook writes it (the wall line, the burn gate and `budget status` still never do). The samples handed to the rate are the same list, so the rate shown is unchanged. On a copy of a real log the prompt notice measured about 0.02 s (median), from about 0.17 s.
