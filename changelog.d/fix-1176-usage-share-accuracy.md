### Fixed

- **The own-share figure no longer depends on what other agents do** (`usage_share`, `usage_meter`, `budget_digest`, policy `credit_budget` §3.1, #1176): the hook line's `me` and `budget me` are this agent's own metered tokens times a frozen calibration, read from the newest `usage_rate_calibrated` event; the hourly refit on the Stop path and `budget rate` are gone. `macf_tools budget calibrate` records the rate as the lower envelope of the account's counter over the agent's own tokens: the smallest of (counter change + 1) / own weighted tokens over every 1 to 12 hour window of the last 14 days, a bound other agents' use can only loosen; it prints the window that set it and how many were considered, and `--dry-run` records nothing. Cache reads weigh 0.1, a constant. When `me` over a window exceeds the account's movement plus one, it is shown uncapped and marked `!` (`me !3.6%/1h`) and `budget me` says the calibration looks stale. The hook line names its window (`me 0.9%/1h`). A metered event's calls are spread across their first and last times instead of credited to the last, and a call copied into a forked subagent's transcript is counted once per session. Each `usage_rolled` event carries the calibration in force, so finding it never walks the log.
