### Fixed

- **The own-share figure no longer depends on what other agents do** (`usage_share`, `usage_meter`, `budget_digest`, policy `credit_budget` §3.1, #1176): the hook line's `me` and `budget me` are this agent's own metered tokens times a frozen calibration, read from the newest `usage_rate_calibrated` event; the hourly refit on the Stop path and `budget rate` are gone. `macf_tools budget calibrate` records the rate as the lower envelope of the account's counter over the agent's own tokens: the smallest of (counter change + 1) / own weighted tokens over every 1 to 12 hour window of the last 14 days, a bound other agents' use can only loosen. The windows are whole hours and step windows, which run from a step-up of the counter to the sample just before a later step-up, where the added one is nearly all real movement, so the minimum over both is tighter and still sound; it prints the kind of window that set the rate (also recorded), its span and how many were considered, and `--dry-run` records nothing. Cache reads weigh 0.1, a constant. When `me` over a window exceeds the account's movement plus one, it is shown uncapped and marked `!` (`me !3.6%/1h`); within six hours of calibrating `budget me` says the calibrated bound is loose for this hour and tightens by recalibrating after heavy hours, and after that it says the calibration looks stale. The hook line names its window (`me 0.9%/1h`). A metered event's calls are spread across their first and last times instead of credited to the last, and a call copied into a forked subagent's transcript is counted once per session. Each `usage_rolled` event carries the calibration in force, so finding it never walks the log.
