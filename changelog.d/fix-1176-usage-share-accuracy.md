### Fixed

- **The hook line can no longer show one agent spending more than the whole account** (`usage_share`, `usage_meter`, `budget_digest`, policy `credit_budget` §3.1, #1176): the share fit now picks the cache-read weight from the counter (a grid from 0 to the price list's 0.1, lowest residual), and every conversion of tokens to points uses the fitted weight; at the fixed 0.1, cache reads were most of the metered total and an hour of parallel subagents came out at 3.9 points while the account moved 2. A share above the account's movement over the same window plus one point is capped and marked (`≤` on the hook line, a line in `budget me`), so "others" never falls below minus one. The hook line names its window (`me 0.9%/1h`) so it is not read against the two-hour smoothed weekly rate. A metered event's calls are spread across their first and last times instead of credited to the last, and a call copied into a forked subagent's transcript is counted once per session.
