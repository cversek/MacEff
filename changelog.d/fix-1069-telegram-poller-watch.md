### Added

- **The outside watch now reports a Telegram poller that ended or went silent** (`pd.watch.check_telegram`, #1069): for a home whose `operator_channels` lists a `plugin:telegram:` channel, the finding `telegram` reads POLLER-DOWN when the pid in the plugin's `bot.pid` is not running, or runs but has written no heartbeat to `diag.jsonl` in two minutes, and CHECK-FAILED when `bot.pid` can't be read. Until now a seat whose poller had exited kept its session while its operator's phone reached no one, and no alert said so. The remedy, `/mcp` in the session, is in the alert and in `persistent_layer` section 4.
