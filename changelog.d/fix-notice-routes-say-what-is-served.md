### Changed

- **A declaration names only notice routes that reach someone** (`pd/interface.py`, `pd/notifier.py`, `pd/daemon.py`, policy `persistent_layer` 1.8, MIS-0002-R42 and R47; the repo owner's ruling of 2026-10-21 on #552, option A). `agent` is served for every source. `operator`, and the operator's half of `both`, is served only for `pd_unread_mail`, by the outside watch. `held_until_present` is served nowhere yet. A declaration that routes a source to `held_until_present`, or to `operator` for anything but unread mail, is now refused at load, naming the route that works; before, it was accepted and the notices dropped. `both` for another source keeps its agent half, and the daemon says at start that the rest is unserved (the head maintainer's review on #552).
  - A notice the daemon raises itself (`pd_carried`, `pd_entry_due`, `pd_duty_due`, `pd_run_failed`, `pd_limit_exceeded`, `pd_schedule_changed`, `pd_grant_denied`) goes to the agent when unrouted. A declaration rendered without routes, as the containers' are, now delivers what was carried after a restart (R47).
  - The three start warnings for an unheard `pd_carried`, `pd_schedule_changed` or `pd_grant_denied` are gone: the refusal at load makes them unreachable.
  - The documented example routes only what is served.
