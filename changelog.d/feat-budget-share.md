### Added

- **An agent's share of the account's weekly counter.** `macf_tools budget rate` fits points per weighted token by regressing the account's hourly change on the agent's own hourly weighted tokens, through the origin, over the last 48 hours (`--window` changes it), and prints the rate with a 90% bootstrap interval, the hours used and r². With fewer than six usable hours it says so and gives no rate. `budget me` now ends with the agent's share of the account's movement over its window, with the interval, and the remainder that other agents and unmetered use spent; the remainder reads unknown, not zero, when the counter has too few samples. The weekly figure on the hook line gains the agent's own last-hour rate, `· me 0.21%/h`, from the newest fit cached as a `usage_rate_fitted` event (the provisional constant until one exists); the hooks read the last hour of metered events and never regress.
