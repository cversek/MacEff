### Fixed

- **A failed budget refresh is waited out instead of retried on every hook call** (`macf/src/macf/budget.py`, policy `credit_budget` §3.1). A failed fetch is now recorded as a `budget_sample_failed` event with a short reason, the HTTP status and any `Retry-After`, and the hooks wait from the last attempt rather than the last success: each failure in a row doubles the wait up to two hours (`MACEFF_BUDGET_BACKOFF_CAP_MIN`), and a reply that says how long to wait is waited out. Before, a failure left nothing behind, so after one 429 every tool call asked the usage endpoint again, which kept it refusing for days while the weekly field aged. The weekly field now says why it is stale and what to run (`wk 38% (57h old: endpoint 429; budget sample --manual)`), and once the sample's week has reset it says so instead of reporting the old percentage. A failed refresh no longer suppresses the band and wall reminders, which now say how old their sample is, and `budget status` names the last failure and the next attempt and no longer counts down to a reset that has already passed (#852).
