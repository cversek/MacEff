### Changed

- **Persistent layer policy answers what a cold reader couldn't** (`framework/policies/base/infrastructure/persistent_layer.md`). Section 10.2 now states what the core does with a crashing unit: what counts as a failure, which restart policies restart what, the backoff from one second doubling to five minutes, the count that clears after ten liveness intervals, and that restarts have no cap. Section 11.1 says where a crash loop shows: `pd_unit_state`, `pd_control` with its failure count, and what `pd status` does and doesn't print. Sections 1.2, 1.5 and 1.8 say that a notice counts as delivered when it is written to the channel, so one the channel refuses or the prompt hook never records isn't sent again, that a daemon notice the channel would refuse is never owed, and that a restarted daemon reads back a day of owed notices. Section 10.6 says how each entry command shows a mark, that a mark stays with its entry, and that a fired entry stays held until it is removed. Each statement was checked against the code.
