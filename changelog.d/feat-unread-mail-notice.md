### Added

- **The primal daemon notices mail left unread past a declared threshold** (`pd/unread_mail.py`, `pd/daemon.py`, `pd/core.py`, `pd/interface.py`, `pd/notifier.py`, `pd/watch.py`, `channels/maceff_channel.py`, policy `persistent_layer` §1.11, MIS-0002-R55): a new `unread_mail_threshold_s` declaration field, which must be routed as the `pd_unread_mail` notice source. Each unread spell gives one notice, from the oldest message passing the threshold until the pickup box is empty. The wait counts only while the agent's session runs, so an agent that wasn't there to read isn't noticed. A spell is recorded as `pd_unread_mail` and `pd_unread_mail_cleared` events and read back by a restarted daemon. The agent's half goes through the MacEff channel; the operator's half through the outside watch.
