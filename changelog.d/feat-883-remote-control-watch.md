### Added

- **Remote Control disconnects are recognized and alerted from outside the session** (`transcript_monitor/remote_control.py`, `pd/watch.py`, #883). The client writes each disconnect into the transcript ("Remote Control disconnected — <reason> — run /login"), and nothing else noticed it, so a session could sit unreachable from other devices for hours. The session's transcript monitor now records `remote_control_disconnected` with the reason, its kind (login, network, ended elsewhere) and the command that fixes it, and `remote_control_remedied` when that command is run: a login that worked, or `/remote-control` for a network drop or a session ended elsewhere. The client records no reconnect, so a disconnect written well after an open one is taken as a new disconnect after an unrecorded reconnect. The outside watch reports an open disconnect as `RC-DISCONNECTED` through its own alert command, once per disconnect, with the remedy and how to reach the session.
