### Added

- **The outside watch compares each named container's limits after every recreate** (`pd/watch.py`, policy `persistent_layer` 7.1; MIS-0002-R61). The comparison of declared and in-force limits existed but ran only by hand. Now the watch config's `containers` lists compose-created containers, and on each pass the watch reads every container's id. A new id means a recreate, however it was done, so the watch compares the limits then and keeps the result with the id. It reports DRIFTED, naming each limit with both values, until the limits match again: one alert per stretch, one recovery line. A comparison that couldn't be made is CHECK-FAILED and is tried again on the next pass. The limits in force are read every pass, so a `docker update` shows at once, and the compose files are read again when one changes, so fixing them closes the stretch. A failed check is its own subject, and both subjects are reported on every pass, so a failure in the middle of a drift opens and recovers its own stretch while the drift stays one stretch with one alert. The `.env` beside the first compose file is watched with the files.
