### Changed

- **Agent mail is judged against the operator's registry, by the broker and the recipient alike** (`amail/attest.py` `sender_verdict`, `amail/zone.py` `card_for_address`, `amail/broker.py`, `amail/client.py`, amail policy §3.2.3, MIS-0004 R44). A sender the registry declares is held to its keys: unsigned mail, or a signature no key valid for it verifies, is marked suspect. Any other sender, and every sender while the registry is missing or untrusted, is unverified; contact books no longer verify signatures. The broker resolves the sender's calling card once (zone token removed, the deployment's `agent_cards`, or a card-form local part) and writes it with its acceptance time into the hand-off sidecar, and the recipient judges by the same card at the same time, so the two never disagree. A receiver's amail settings may name its `domain`, which the daemon's receiver and `amail list` pass to ingest.
