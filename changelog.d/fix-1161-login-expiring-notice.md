### Added

- **A daemon warns its agent before its Claude login ends** (`pd/login.py`, `pd/daemon.py`, `pd/notifier.py`, `channels/maceff_channel.py`, policy `persistent_layer` §4 and §6.2, #1161): the daemon already recorded `pd_login_state` every hour, but only the outside watch and the declared report times acted on it. A container seat has neither, and one seat's login expired overnight with no warning. The daemon now raises a `pd_login_expiring` notice to its agent at three steps: the warning window (`reports.warn_days`, three days by default, with or without a declaration), one day before the end, and the end. Each step is recorded as `pd_login_warned` before its notice and is warned once. A restart reads back the last warning, and a renewed login starts its steps again. The notice asks the agent to tell the operator, since only a person can run `/login`. Eleven tests; nine planted defects each turn at least one red, including the three wiring gaps and the arrival-id survivor the maintainers found by planting. On a login several agents share on macOS the check reads unknown, so no agent there is warned; the policy says so.
