### Added

- **Rung 2 of the delivery ladder: a broker hands mail to a peer broker over a private network** (`amail/peer_link.py`, `Broker.hand_off_peer`, `take_over_link`, `flush_outbox`, `peer_links` and `peer_listen` in the broker configuration, MIS-0004 R56 to R64, amail policy 2.1.2): the sender connects to the receiver's listener and keeps the message until the receiver acknowledges it; the receiver binds one declared private address, closes an address no declared peer holds before reading, reads a request only up to a bound and within a deadline, writes the pair into its own peer intake and answers only then, and its contact list, keys and quarantine decide what reaches an agent. A message with no acknowledgement stays in the sender's outbox and is retried. Nothing listens unless `peer_listen` is declared.
