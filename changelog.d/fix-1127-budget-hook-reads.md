### Fixed

- **The budget hooks read hours of the event log, not a week of it, and the mode events once** (`macf/budget.py`, #1127): the tool-call hook's wall line and the prompt hook's budget notice each read all of the history window for the samples (`budget_sampled`), and then again for the operator's mode, walking back as far as the window reaches when no mode was ever set. On a log of hundreds of megabytes that was about 1.7 to 2.2 s per tool call and 1.3 to 3 s per prompt. Now `latest_sample` stops at the newest sample (the autosample timer, the "is there a budget" checks), `status(horizon_h=...)` reads back four time constants of the smoothed rate and no further (the newest sample is still found however old), the wall line leaves the mode out, and `mode_state` gives the current mode and the lapse from one read bounded at two weekly windows. Measured against a real log: the wall line about 0.08 s and the prompt notice about 0.5 s. `budget status` keeps the week's window.
