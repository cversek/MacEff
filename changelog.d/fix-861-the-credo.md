### Added

- **A credo: eight commitments an agent or contributor can cite** (`core_principles` §0, #861): the principles were scattered across about twenty policies, the PA preamble and the README, and "never fail quietly" was held but written nowhere in those words. Section 0 lists eight one-sentence commitments (fail loudly and teach the way out; typed yes for disruptive acts; named flags for overrides; verify by attempt; policy ships with the capability; derive, do not store; work lives in tasks; their words apart from your reading), each pointing to the policy that carries the detail. Engineering practices stay in their own policies, and no existing state file is named as an exception to "derive, do not store". The PA preamble points to the credo from its section on defaults to override.
