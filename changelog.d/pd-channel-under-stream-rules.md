### Changed

- **The operator's stream rules now reach the agent's session** (MIS-0002 R152, policy `persistent_layer` 11.4, 1.8 and 1.9). The session is a named subscriber in the event stream's rules, `@channel`. Before the notifier writes a notice to the MacEff channel, or types a keystroke wake for mail, it asks the daemon's live rules whether the notice's source is delivered to `channel`. So `pd stream silence amail` stops mail notices to the session as well as to the stream's subscribers, `pd stream silence @channel` stops every notice to it, and `pd stream route <source> <subscriber>` keeps that source from it. No stream subscriber may name itself `channel`.
- **A silenced notice is settled, not owed.** It is recorded as `pd_notice_silenced` and dropped from what is owed. A restart reads it back as settled, as it reads `pd_notice_delivered`, and taking the rule away later never delivers it. An entry's wake silenced this way is not owed again past a day either.
