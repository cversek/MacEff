### Fixed

- **The MacEff channel keeps reconnecting to its daemon after any error** (`channels/maceff_channel.py`, policy `persistent_layer` §1.8, #1156): the channel's link to the daemon runs in its own thread, and an exception that escaped it, such as the reset of a daemon that died mid-connection, ended the thread for good while the process went on serving the session. The client still listed the channel, and every notice fell to the keystroke fallback for four days. Each failed attempt is now written to stderr and retried with the existing back-off. A connection reset mid-stream ends that connection only, and what it delivered still counts. Three tests; two planted defects each turn one red.
- **A wake held by a screen with no input box says so, instead of "dialog"** (`pd/wake.py`, `pd/watch.py`, policy `persistent_layer` §1.9, #1156): the keystroke fallback reported any screen without an input box as a dialog, so text written over the client's display (a system broadcast every five minutes) held mail wakes for a day under the reason `dialog`, and the outside watch alerted the operator about a dialog that wasn't there. Such a screen is now `no_box`. It still holds, failing closed. Its screen is kept, and the watch's alert says what it means and points at the channel. A dialog drawn in the box's place, such as a permission prompt, is still `dialog`, recognized by its markers or numbered options. Three tests and updated expectations; five planted defects each turn one red.
