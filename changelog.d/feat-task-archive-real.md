### Added

- **`task archive` works again, and `task unarchive` reverses it** (`macf/src/macf/task/archive.py`, `cli.py`, policy `task_management` §7): `task archive ID [ID ...] --reason TEXT` sets each task and its descendants to status `archived`, records the reason and the status each had, takes them out of sprint scope, names any task a role duty tracks, and logs one `task_archived` event. Every change is read back before it is counted, and a write that did not stick is named and exits 1, the opposite of the command retired on 2026-08-06 for printing success while writing nothing. `--dry-run` lists and changes nothing; `--no-cascade` stops at the named tasks. `task unarchive` puts each task back at its recorded status. Nothing is copied to `task_archives/`.

### Changed

- **An archived subtree is one 📦 line in `task tree`** (`cli.py`): with a count of the archived tasks beneath it, and any task left open under it still drawn. `--succinct` draws no archived work; `--all` draws it expanded and dimmed; `--archived` draws only archived tasks and the ancestors that lead to them (it used to fail with "Task #000 not found"). The tree, `task list` and `--json` now agree on what archived means: status `archived` or the legacy MTMD flag. `task restore` and `task archived list` stay retired and now point at `task unarchive` and `task tree --archived`.
- **`task tree` shows notes by verbosity level** (`cli.py`, policy `task_management`): by default each task's notes are a count on its own line (📝 N) instead of a line each, completion reports are not shown, and a subtask's plan line is left out (a GitHub issue or PR keeps its link line); `-v` / `--verbose` shows what the default used to (notes, plans and reports listed, truncated); `-vv` / `--verbose 2` shows every note, plan, report, breadcrumb and lifecycle update in full, wrapped to 80% of the terminal's width.
