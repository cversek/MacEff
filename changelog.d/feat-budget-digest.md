### Added

- **An hourly spend digest, sprint budget records and the operator's intent on the budget line** (`macf/src/macf/budget_digest.py`, `budget status`): the prompt notice adds a short block at most once an hour, and only when the agent metered new spend since the last one, giving the hour's points, the top three causes, the largest driver (context × turns when cache reads dominate), the agent's share of the account's movement and what one more turn costs at the current context. A SPRINT task records `sprint_budget_start` when created and `sprint_budget_end` with the account and agent deltas when completed, printed and added as a task note; a failure to record warns and never blocks the task. `budget status` shows the mode with who set it and when, and a lapsed mode with its date.
