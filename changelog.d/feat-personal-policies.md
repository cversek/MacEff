### Added

- **Personal policies are reached like framework policies** (PA preamble v1.9): `personal:<name>` needs no setup, `policy search` and `recommend` reach every root, `personal/manifest.json` declares which surface at `task start`, and `policy check` reports what a policy lacks against the format.

### Changed

- **Personal policies augment; they never replace.** `policy_awareness`, `core_principles`, `checkpoints` and the template said they override all others, which nothing enforced.
