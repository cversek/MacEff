### Added

- **The mail ports as an allow-list in containers** (`amail/egress.py`, `docker/scripts/start.py`, `models/agent_spec.py`, policy `capability_boundaries` §3.2, MIS-0004 R33). A new `mail_egress.allow_accounts` in agents.yaml names who may open the mail ports (25, 465, 587, 993). The start step installs an ACCEPT per listed account, matched on the socket's owner, then a REJECT for every other owner, for IPv4 and IPv6, and reads the chain back. It refuses a list that names an account that doesn't exist, leaves out `amail_broker` when the image has it, or names an account also denied a mail port. Without the field, nothing changes: the per-agent `deny_tcp_ports` lists apply as before, and start logs that R33 isn't met, because a deny-list leaves every unnamed account free to send. An effect probe in a multi-seat container found five service accounts that could reach port 587 while the three seats were refused.
