### Added

- **An agent paces its own keystroke wake** (`pd/interface.py`, `pd/daemon.py`, `pd/notifier.py`, `pd/wake.py`, `pd/cli.py`, policy `persistent_layer` §1.9 and §12.3, `macf/docs/developer/primal_daemon_interface.md`).
  - **The command:** `macf_tools pd wake --refractory 5m --reason "<why>"` sets how long the wake rests after each wake. `--declared` goes back to the declared window, and `pd wake` alone shows the window in force and where it came from.
  - **The declaration:** `wake_refractory_s` (default 600, the ten minutes the operator kept) and an optional `wake_refractory_floor_s`, which no pace goes under, and `wake_refractory_ceiling_s`, which no pace goes over (default 1800, the watch's allowance for a held wake, so no pace can mute the wake past the point where the operator would be told). Only the declaration sets the floor and the ceiling, never the control socket; in a container, root renders the declaration. No window may be infinite or not a number: `inf` is refused at the command line, the socket and the declaration.
  - **On the socket:** a `pace` request, asked by the new `agent` asker kind or by the operator. It acts on no unit. It is recorded like every act (`pd_control` with act `pace` and an empty unit, the asker, the reason and the asking process), and in `pd_wake_paced` with the window it replaced, before the daemon answers. A pace that can't be recorded isn't set.
  - **Across restarts:** the next daemon reads the newest `pd_wake_paced` of its agent back at its start and keeps it, naming where it came from. A floor declared above it, or a ceiling below it, since then wins, and the daemon says so; a saved pace that isn't finite is held to the ceiling.
  - A status reply carries the window, wherever a session unit has a keystroke wake.
