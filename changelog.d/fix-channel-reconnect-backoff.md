### Fixed

- **A refused MacEff channel backs off instead of spinning** (`channels/maceff_channel.py`). A primal daemon refuses a channel outside its live session's lineage by accepting the connection and closing it at once (MIS-0002-R122). The channel took that as a connection, reset its back-off and reconnected with no pause: about 600 connections a second, measured live when a second session's channel met the check, and one journal line per refusal. Any Claude session under the agent's login starts the plugin's server, so every stray session would have done this. The channel now always waits before reconnecting, and only a connection that delivered a notice or held 10 s resets the delay. Two tests; two planted defects each fail them. Live, the same refused channel made 3 attempts in 7 s.
