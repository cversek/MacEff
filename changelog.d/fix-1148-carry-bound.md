### Fixed

- **The carried samples are capped at 12 hours and keep a sample written while they were carried** (`macf/budget.py`, #1148): a `budget_samples_carried` event holds every sample of its horizon (about 1.2 KB an hour, written hourly), so its size grew with the rate's time constant, to about 13 MB a day at the 8-day history cap. A horizon over `MAX_CARRY_HORIZON_H` (12 hours, room for a time constant of up to three) is now read from the log as before and not carried, so the rate is the same and the event stays under about 15 KB. The event also records `through`, the newest sample time it holds, and a read continues past the carry for `budget_sampled` events newer than that, so a sample another process appended while the carrier was writing is no longer lost to later reads.
