# MIS-0004 section 12.4 inventory: the host-side half

One row per normative statement in the amail policy's host-side areas (addressing and the tier, the delivery ladder, outbound mail, the host tier, credentials), with what cites it and a proposed disposition. A first pass for the PRs that retire clauses to work from; a row marked "rewrite?" is a question, not a ruling.


82 rows, read from origin/main of the amail policy (v1.14.0) and MIS-0004. The policy itself carries no clause IDs, so every row is cited by section and first words ("sec" stands for the section sign, kept ASCII). Clause IDs of the form O5b.6 or O5f.3 come from the 0.x working specification, which is not in the repository; where code cites one that matches a policy statement it is shown beside that row, and four in-scope spec IDs with no policy counterpart get rows of their own at the end. Cited-by lists only places that cite the clause (by section or spec ID); tests that exercise a behavior without citing it are named in parentheses as "uncited". Ambiguous: the brief says R01..R49 but MIS-0004 stops at R46; MIS-0004's R06 text says "from the calling card" and the zone token exists only in code and changelog (test_amail_zone_token.py:1 calls it "R06 revised"); the one-zone rule and the posture concept live in MIS Goals and Terms, not in any R.

Statements I could not place:

- ratelimit.py:46 cites spec O5g.5 for "notices are composed by nobody, so they cannot be charged to an agent", while sec 6b.3 says notices escaping the rate limit was the hole. Whether notices draw on a broker budget or none needs a reading of the code, not the comments.
- sec 6b.1 (sender's copy, two ids) and sec 6b.2 (broker-owned disposition history) are outbound but about custody and audit, not submission or transport. Left for the hand-off and audit half; R15 and R21 to R24 touch them.
- sec 6b.4 (never bounce to an unauthenticated sender) and the O5g.* family cited in notices.py and inbound.py: outbound in form, inbound in trigger. Left to the inbound half.
- sec 6b.5 (a key arriving in a message is a claim): contacts and signing, joint area; R42 likely supersedes it.
- sec 2.3 "The recipient is told where its box is" (handoff and agent keys in amail.json, never the environment) reads like addressing and R06's no-environment rule, but it sits in the hand-off section. Not inventoried here.
- sec 3.1 "The contact restriction MUST be enforced by a process the agent does not control": broker enforcement, other half, though 6b.0 restates it on the outbound side.
- The 0.x working specification itself is not in the repository, so spec IDs outside the code's citations (any O5b.*, O5e.*, O5f.* never cited) cannot be listed. Whoever holds that series should diff it against this table.
